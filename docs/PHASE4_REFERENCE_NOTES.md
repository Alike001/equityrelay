# Phase 4 reference notes

EquityRelay reviewed the public Shijima repository for presentation patterns before the Phase 4 judge experience work.

Patterns adapted at the product-design level:

- a short, curated journey from product behavior to independently inspectable evidence;
- explicit user-triggered checks with distinct unavailable and mismatch states;
- status views that show `NOT_CHECKED` when no recent observation exists;
- separate labels for live data, historical canonical evidence, and recorded decisions;
- direct links from claims to proof surfaces and public explorers.

No Shijima code, copy, branding, imagery, or other assets were incorporated. The repository's MIT license and third-party notices were reviewed and respected.
